The Possession Dilemma

The rush of handover day conceals the flaws you inherit.

Handovers are typically fast-paced, cosmetic walk-throughs conducted under pressure. Fresh paint frequently masks underlying construction deviations that become costly repairs after move-in.

01 / CONCEALED DEFECTS

Acoustic Hollow Tiles

Poor adhesive bonding leaves void spaces under vitrified tiles, leading to cracked corners and loose floorboards within months.

02 / WATERPROOFING & GRADIENT

Shower & Balcony Slope Traps

Incorrect tile slope in wet areas prevents natural drainage toward floor traps, creating chronic water stagnation and perimeter seepage.

03 / ELECTRICAL SAFETY

Phase-Neutral Reversals

Invisible wiring errors, missing earthing on heavy appliance points, and non-tripping RCCB circuits leave appliances vulnerable.

Comprehensive Coverage

Room-by-room, system-by-system scrutiny.

Our scopes cover both the visible architectural finishes and the functional utilities that support daily living.

Flooring & Tiles

Hollow acoustic checks, lippage unevenness, grouting continuity, slope verification toward drains.

Electrical Systems

Socket polarity, grounding verification, MCB/RCCB trip testing, switch plate alignments.

Plumbing & Sanitary

Trap seals, slow drainage diagnostics, water pressure drops, angle cock leaks, flush tank mechanism integrity.

Doors & Windows

Sash plumbness, silicone weather sealing, latch engagements, glass scratch audits, hinge clearances.

Walls & Ceilings

Hairline shrinkage cracks, dampness moisture readings, plaster undulations, paint unevenness.

Safety & Balconies

Railing anchor rigidity, parapet height compliance, glass balustrade vibration, child safety latch clearances.
