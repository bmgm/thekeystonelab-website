The Possession Dilemma

The rush of handover day conceals the flaws you inherit.

Handovers are typically fast-paced, cosmetic walk-throughs conducted under pressure. Fresh paint frequently masks underlying construction deviations that become costly repairs after move-in.

01 / CONCEALED DEFECTS

Acoustic Hollow Tiles

Poor adhesive bonding leaves void spaces under vitrified or ceramic tiles, leading to cracked corners and loose floorboards within months.

02 / WATERPROOFING & GRADIENT

Shower & Balcony Slope Traps

Incorrect tile slope in wet areas prevents natural drainage toward floor traps, creating chronic water stagnation and perimeter seepage.

03 / ELECTRICAL SAFETY

Phase-Neutral Reversals

Invisible wiring errors, missing earthing on heavy appliance points, and non-tripping RCCB circuits leave appliances vulnerable to surges.

Comprehensive Coverage

Room-by-room, system-by-system scrutiny.

Our inspection scopes cover both the visible architectural finishes and the functional utilities that support daily living.

Flooring & Tiles

Hollow acoustic checks, lippage unevenness, grouting continuity, slope verification toward drains, skirt alignment.

Electrical Systems

Socket polarity, grounding verification, MCB/RCCB trip testing, switch plate alignments, concealed conduit checks.

Plumbing & Sanitary

Trap seals, slow drainage diagnostics, water pressure drops, angle cock leaks, flush tank mechanism integrity.

Doors & Windows

Sash plumbness, silicone weather sealing, latch engagements, glass scratch audits, hinge friction and clearances.

Walls & Ceilings

Hairline shrinkage cracks, dampness moisture reading, plaster undulations, paint unevenness, drywall joints.

Safety & Balconies

Railing anchor rigidity, parapet height compliance, glass balustrade vibration, child safety latch clearances.

Hollow Tile Void Under Footfall Path

Attention

Acoustic tapping survey identified a ~0.45 sq.m void space beneath two 800x800mm vitrified tiles near the en-suite doorway threshold. Inadequate adhesive mortar bed.
