What We Inspect

Civil & Construction

Surface condition, visible cracks, alignment, finishing quality, and accessible construction elements.

Flooring & Tiles

Tile condition, surface damage, grout quality, level and finish issues, and joint alignment.

Walls & Ceilings

Visible cracks, dampness indicators, paint finish, surface defects, and overall condition.

Doors

Alignment, operation, locks, handles, hinges, frames, and installation quality.

Windows

Operation, alignment, locks, handles, glass condition, sealing, and visible issues.

Electrical

Switches, sockets, fixtures, distribution board accessibility, and observable safety concerns.

Plumbing

Faucets, water flow, drainage, visible leakage, fixtures, and accessible connections.

Bathrooms

Sanitary fixtures, drainage, tile and grout, sealant, water flow, and ventilation.

Kitchen

Countertop, sink, plumbing, cabinets, hardware, tiles, and electrical points.

Fixtures & Fittings

Handles, hardware, sanitary fittings, light fixtures, and installation quality.

Safety & Accessibility

Observable safety concerns, access to service points, and basic usability observations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an apartment inspection? ▼
An apartment inspection is a systematic review of the accessible and observable components of a property to identify defects, workmanship concerns, installation issues, and other observable problems. Unlike a casual walkthrough, a professional inspection uses a structured checklist and systematic approach to ensure consistent coverage of important areas.
When should I get my apartment inspected? ▼
Common timing includes: before possession (when possible), during handover walkthrough, before interior work begins, or before moving into the property. Inspecting before possession allows you to document issues while the property is still in the builder's responsibility.
What areas do you inspect? ▼
We inspect 11 major categories: Civil & Construction, Flooring & Tiles, Walls & Ceilings, Doors, Windows, Electrical, Plumbing, Bathrooms, Kitchen, Fixtures & Fittings, and Safety & Accessibility. The exact scope depends on your property type, accessibility, and service package.
Will I receive a report? ▼
Yes. You receive a structured digital inspection report that organizes all findings by room and category, with severity levels, descriptions, and photographic evidence. The report is easy to review, share with builders or contractors, and reference later.
How do I book an inspection? ▼
You can book directly through our website using the "Book Inspection" button, or contact us with your property details and preferred inspection date. We'll confirm availability and send you next steps.
